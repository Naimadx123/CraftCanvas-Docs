## Graphical menus from YAML

CraftCanvas uses signed books, Adventure components and resource-pack glyphs to build graphical menus on Paper and Folia **1.21.11 through 26.3**. Players use a matching vanilla client and accept your resource pack. Nexo, CraftEngine and ItemsAdder are optional.

<CardGrid stagger>
  <Card title="Build with your own artwork" icon="pencil">
    Position PNGs, text and controls with YAML. Reuse themes and templates across screens.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Make menus interactive" icon="setting">
    Add selection states, tooltips, variables, conditions, navigation and server actions.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Bring your resource pack" icon="open-book">
    Generate a standalone pack or integrate with Nexo, CraftEngine and ItemsAdder.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Extend with Java or Kotlin" icon="puzzle">
